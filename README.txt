leanengineer -- class notes of Von Glurt
========================================================================

Personal class notes by Von Glurt (Paul Richeson), begun 2026-11-25.
MIT licensed (own notes only; see LICENSE and DEC-00, rule R-10).

PURPOSE

IEEE-style, plain-ASCII notes on the Lean 4 theorem prover, taken
from the public lecture series "Lean for Scientists and Engineers,
Summer 2024" by Tyler Josephson, together with analysis of what the
material means and where it leads. Written for an expert engineering
reader: precise, referenced, with every source's assumptions stated.
