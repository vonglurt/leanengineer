Abstract--Project P01 follows eleven recorded lectures, 20 h 41 min
of video in all, budgeted here as about 15 hours of lecture
exposition, taken with breaks, and workshop time for following along
in Lean and for individual exploration. This syllabus lists the
lectures with their running times, topics, and readings; fixes the
shape of a session; and states the assumptions behind the time
budget, which the author is asked to confirm.

Index Terms--syllabus, Lean 4, lecture series, time budget, workshop.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
I. INTRODUCTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The lecture series is an introduction to Lean for non-mathematicians,
given at UMBC in July and August 2024 and published on YouTube in
2025 [1]. Its description names three subjects: logic and proofs for
scientists and engineers; functional programming in Lean 4; and
provably correct programs for scientific computing [2]. Slides and
Lean code are said to be in the course repository [3].

This syllabus serves the audience of DEC-00, Section III: lecture
content is recorded by timestamp and reference, not re-taught.

IV. SHAPE OF A SESSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

One session per lecture, eleven in all. Each runs the cycle of SP-00,
Fig. 2, in these blocks.

1) Prepare (15 min). Read the lecture's chapter list. Mark which
   chapters are exposition and which are follow-along. Reread the
   open actions and the open questions likely to come up.

2) Lecture (exposition chapters). View in segments of 45 minutes or
   less, with a 10-minute break at a chapter boundary. While viewing,
   take timestamps of three kinds:
     - a concept to research ...... becomes a research topic, RT-nn
     - a point for discussion ..... goes to Section V of the report
     - a slide worth keeping ...... becomes a figure ("make shot")
   Each timestamp is recorded with its share link (DEC-00, R-03).

3) Workshop, follow-along (follow-along chapters). Reproduce the
   lecturer's Lean work in the P01 workspace; pause as needed.

4) Workshop, individual exploration (1 h or more). In this order of
   priority: open the session's research topics, questions first
   (DEC-00, R-01); do the exercises of the current phase (SP-00,
   TABLE III); note ideas for side projects (DEC-00, R-07).

5) Record (within 24 h). Session report, edited chat log, critical
   review (SP-00, Sections IV and VI).

The self-study phases of SP-00, TABLE III, are the backbone of the
individual exploration. Where their order differs from the lectures,
the lectures govern.

from the course's own schedule. Every figure in Section III names its
source or is labeled as an assumption.

B. Weaknesses and Risks

1) The mapping of schedule rows to videos is by order, confirmed for

   the other nine lectures have video files but no transcripts yet.
4) The course repository [3] is known from the video description and
