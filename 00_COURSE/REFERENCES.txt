------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each source is formatted once here, in IEEE style, under a permanent
KEY. A report copies the entry into its own reference list and numbers
it [1], [2], ... in order of first citation. Figure and table files
cite by [KEY].

Status values:
  UNVERIFIED  Not yet checked against the source. Do not quote a page,
              title, or claim from it until it is checked.
  VERIFIED    Checked against the source; access date recorded.
  SUPERSEDED  A newer edition or revision exists; see Notes.

Every entry of Revision A was compiled on 2026-11-25 with the help of
an AI assistant, from its training knowledge, without opening the
sources (SP-00, Section IX-B; BP-03; action SP-00/A-02). Entries
added in Revision B state individually how they were checked.

Each entry also records what the source assumes (DEC-00, R-02;
BP-14). "Assumes : --" means not yet recorded; it is filled in when
the source is verified or first used in an argument.

Entry form:

  KEY        :
  Entry      : <IEEE format>
  Status     :
  Accessed   : <Mon. DD, YYYY, for online sources>
  Cited in   : <Document ID [n]>
  Assumes    : <what the source takes for granted>
  Notes      :
